A three-set Venn diagram of Q3 customer segments, email subscribers, paid users and mobile app users, with the number of users in each of the seven regions; each number counts the users in exactly that combination of segments, 29,810 users in all. It follows John Venn's 1880 form ("On the Diagrammatic and Mechanical Representation of Propositions and Reasonings", Philosophical Magazine), in which every combination of sets is drawn as a region whether or not it holds anything.

Why it works as the exemplar for this type:

- Three equal circles with their centres on an equilateral triangle, so all seven regions exist and the three two-way overlaps have the same shape; no region is left out because its count is small.
- Each count is centred at the point of its region farthest from every outline, in semibold ink, a size larger for the single-set regions than for the overlaps; nothing points into a region from outside.
- Set names sit outside the circles, in the set's own colour, with the set total ("15,810 users") as a muted second line: the top set above its circle, the lower two below-left and below-right, all well clear of the title.
- Fills are translucent (about 14%) so overlaps darken where sets combine, and each circle keeps a solid 2px outline in its colour so every boundary can be traced through the overlaps.
- The subtitle states what the numbers mean (exact-region counts and the total), which removes the most common misreading of a counted Venn.
