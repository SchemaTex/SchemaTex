A launch program for a SaaS platform release, January to June 2026: four workstreams (Engineering, Design, Marketing, Customer Success) on one shared calendar, two phases (Build, Launch), two program milestones (Feature freeze, Public launch) and one single-day event (Help center live). It follows the swimlane roadmap convention used in project-management and product-roadmap tools: a lane header column on the left, time running left to right across a calendar header, and each lane holding its own work spans. Milestones are drawn as diamonds, the symbol the Gantt tradition uses for zero-duration events (Wallace Clark, *The Gantt Chart: A Working Tool of Management*, 1922; https://archive.org/details/ganttchartworkin00claruoft).

Why it works as the exemplar for this type:

- The axis is a real calendar: a year band ("2026") over named month columns, with light month gridlines running down through every lane. Ticks are never all labelled with the same year.
- Each lane has a bold name and a small colour swatch in a fixed header column, and alternate lanes get a faint band, so it is always clear which track a bar belongs to.
- A bar's label sits inside the bar when it fits with 10 px padding. When it does not fit, the label goes just to the right of the bar in ink, and anything that would collide moves down to a second row in the same lane.
- Phases sit in their own row under the calendar, and milestones sit in their own row with a diamond, a bold name and the date. The dashed milestone line runs down through every lane, so the handoff date can be read against each workstream. Labels in this row never share horizontal space.
- Gridlines and milestone lines are cut wherever they would pass through text, and every bar keeps a pale tint with a darker outline of the same hue, so labels stay in dark ink and readable.
- A one-line legend at the bottom explains the bar, the circle (one-day event) and the diamond.

Palette: ink #1F2933, muted text #5F6B76, rules #D5DAE0, gridlines #E3E7EB, lane band #F5F7F9, milestone red #B42318, track hues blue #2F6DB5 on #DDE8F5, teal #2A8475 on #D9EEEA, amber #A86A12 on #F5E6CB, plum #76559F on #E8E1F2.
