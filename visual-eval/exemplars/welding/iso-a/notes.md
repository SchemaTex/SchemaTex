A frame weld detail sheet with three welding callouts drawn to ISO 2553:2019 system A. A stiffener to base plate joint has a 5 mm throat fillet, 100 mm long, on the arrow side and a 4 mm throat fillet on the other side, welded with process 135 (MAG). A butt joint has a V-groove from the arrow side with 10 mm penetration, and its tail reads 111/ISO 5817-B/PA: manual metal arc, quality level B, flat position. A lug has symmetric 6 mm leg fillets on both sides, welded with process 135.

Why it works as the exemplar for this type:

- System A uses two lines: a solid reference line plus a parallel dashed identification line drawn just below it. A symbol touching the solid line means the arrow side. A symbol hanging from the dashed line means the other side.
- When the weld is symmetric about the joint (the lug), the dashed line is left out and the two symbols mirror each other across the solid line.
- Size prefixes are written exactly as ISO does: a for fillet throat thickness, z for fillet leg length, s for butt weld penetration. The size goes to the left of the symbol and the length to the right. There is no AWS-style angle or root-opening text inside the symbol.
- The tail fork carries ISO process numbers and the tail order process / quality level / welding position, separated by slashes, with no bracket.
- Joint sketches, straight leaders with filled arrowheads, a bold caption and one plain-English line match the AWS exemplar. The two sheets therefore differ only where the standards differ.
