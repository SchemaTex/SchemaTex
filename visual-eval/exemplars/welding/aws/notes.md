A bracket assembly sheet with three welding callouts drawn to AWS A2.4 (Standard Symbols for Welding, Brazing, and Nondestructive Examination), which is compatible with ISO 2553 system B. A stiffener to base plate joint has an intermittent 8 mm fillet at 50-150 on the arrow side and a continuous 6 mm fillet on the other side. A pipe support is welded all around in the field with a 6 mm fillet. A splice plate butt joint has a V-groove from the arrow side (12 mm deep, 60° angle, 3 mm root opening, ground flush) on a backing bar.

Why it works as the exemplar for this type:

- Each callout sits beside a simple section sketch of its joint. A straight leader with a filled arrowhead runs from the left end of the reference line to the exact weld it describes, and the leader never crosses a part outline.
- There is one solid reference line per callout. Arrow-side weld symbols hang below the line and other-side symbols stand above it. The fillet's perpendicular leg is always on the left.
- Dimensions sit in their fixed places: size to the left of the symbol, length-pitch to the right, root opening inside the groove symbol, and groove angle just beyond its opening. Beyond that come the flush contour bar and the finish letter (G), stacked outward in that order and never touching the symbol.
- Supplementary symbols sit at the junction of the arrow and the reference line: an open circle for weld all around, and a filled flag on a short pole for a field weld. The backing symbol is a small rectangle on the other side, opposite the groove.
- The tail is an open fork at the right end with the process text (GMAW, SMAW; E7018) clear of the fork. Under each sketch, a bold caption and one muted plain-English line say what the callout means.

Palette: ink #20272B (parts, reference lines, symbols, dimensions), muted #59636B (subtitle, captions, centre line), paper #FFFFFF.
