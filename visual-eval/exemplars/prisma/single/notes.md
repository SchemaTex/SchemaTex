A PRISMA 2020 flow diagram for a new systematic review of drug efficacy in refractory migraine that searched only bibliographic databases (MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL) and one trial register (ClinicalTrials.gov). It is drawn to the official PRISMA 2020 template for new systematic reviews that include searches of databases and registers only (Page MJ et al., BMJ 2021;372:n71). The counts reconcile at every step: 1,540 identified, 340 removed, 1,200 screened, 940 excluded, 260 reports sought, 14 not retrieved, 246 assessed, 204 excluded with reasons, 42 studies in 46 reports included.

Why it works as the exemplar for this type:

- Box wording is the template's own wording, word for word, and follows the template's order: identification, removal before screening, screening, retrieval, eligibility, inclusion. Retrieval ("Reports sought for retrieval" / "Reports not retrieved") is its own row, not folded into the screening box.
- Databases and registers are counted separately inside "Records identified from:", with per-source counts in a lighter second line; the removal box lists all three removal reasons, including zero counts.
- One gold header names the pipeline, and light-blue rounded bars on the left name the phases Identification, Screening (spanning three rows) and Included.
- Main-flow boxes form one column with straight downward arrows; each exclusion box sits to the right in the same row, at the same height, joined by a short horizontal arrow.
- "Reports excluded:" lists every reason with its count, and the reasons add up to the box total.
- It uses the same type scale, box style and colours as the dual-pipeline exemplar, so the two variants read as one family.

Palette: ink #1F2933, muted text #52606D, box border #616E7C, arrow #3E4C59, header gold #F5C84C, phase bar blue #CFE0F1, paper #FFFFFF.
