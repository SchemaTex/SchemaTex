A legal entity structure chart for a family office built around a dynasty trust: Robert Smith gifts assets to a South Dakota irrevocable dynasty trust administered by a directed trustee company; the trust makes distributions to the children's separate shares, owns all of a Delaware holding corporation, and holds a 99% limited-partner interest in a family partnership whose 1% general-partner interest the holding company owns; the holding company also owns two single-member LLCs that are disregarded for tax purposes. Shapes follow the U.S. tax structure-chart convention used in IRS Large Business and International (LB&I) International Practice Units and in Big-4 tax structure charts: corporation = rectangle, partnership = triangle, disregarded entity = oval inside a rectangle, trust = oval, individual = circle.

Why it works as the exemplar for this type:

- Shape carries tax classification, and a pastel fill repeats it so types read at a glance: corporation = light-blue rectangle, disregarded entity = light-green rounded rectangle with a clearly inset oval, partnership = pale-amber triangle, trust = lavender oval, individual = peach circle. A slate outline on every shape keeps the chart legible when printed in black and white, and a legend shows each shape in its colour.
- Every entity has the same height and a similar width, so no type dominates the page. The partnership is a wide, low triangle with its name on two lines in the lower two-thirds and the word "Partnership" just below the base; every label inside a shape is checked against that shape's real outline.
- Ownership lines are plain solid lines with no arrowheads. They leave the owner at its bottom centre and enter the owned entity at its top centre (the partnership at its apex), with the percentage in semibold beside the vertical just above the owned entity. Subsidiaries of the same owner share one horizontal bus, with a small dot at each junction.
- Lines from different owners never share a segment or a dot, so each owner's lines read as its own. The partnership's two owners arrive as two separate lines that meet only at the apex: the trust's "99% LP" line comes straight down from above, and the holding company's "1% GP" line leaves the side of the holding company in its own lane, drops to the apex's height and runs a short horizontal stretch into the apex from the left. Every ownership line stays horizontal or vertical; "1% GP" sits above that short run and "99% LP" to the right of the vertical.
- Relationships that are not ownership look different and have arrowheads: fiduciary control is dashed blue ("administers"), and a gift or distribution is dotted brown; every label sits in the clear above or beside its line, never on it.
- Jurisdictions are pale dashed frames with a small spaced uppercase name in the top-left corner (SOUTH DAKOTA, DELAWARE). Both frames share the same width and hug their entities with even padding; the individual stays outside every frame, and the trust's ownership bus runs in the gap between the two frames.
