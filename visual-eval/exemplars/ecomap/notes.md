# Ecomap exemplar — The Okafor Household

**Scenario.** A social worker's assessment of a two-parent household raising a child with
additional needs: Daniel and Amara, their son Chidi (10, the child the case is about) and
daughter Zora (6). Nine outside systems surround them — grandparents, school, developmental
pediatrics, speech/OT therapy, Amara's employer, the disability benefits office, the housing
authority, the parish, and a parent support group. The point of the drawing is that the family's
strength comes from kin and community while two government systems are the stress.

**Centre.** Hartman's classic family centre: a dashed circle holding a mini genogram — square
for male, circle for female, age inside the symbol, name outside, a couple line joining the
parents and a sibship line dropping to the children. Chidi's square is shaded in the health tint
so the identified child is findable at a glance. All spokes stop at the dashed boundary, never
at an individual inside it.

**Palette.** Ink `#1e293b` for lines and labels, slate `#64748b` for secondary notes, and one
accent, `#2563eb`, used only for the household boundary. Six muted category tints, each drawn as
a ring plus a 6%-strength fill: family `#8a6a55`, education `#a8792e`, health `#b4544a`,
work `#3f7d6a`, government `#5f7391`, community `#7a6595`. Stressful ties borrow the health clay
`#b4544a` — the only place colour carries meaning on a line, and it only reinforces the shape.

**Type scale.** Title 23 semibold / subtitle 12.5 / system label 12.5 / caption and legend 11 /
legend heading 10 letterspaced. One font stack throughout.

**Line codes.** Meaning lives in the drawn form, not in colour: strong = three parallel 2px
lines, moderate = two, ordinary = one, tenuous = a single 1.3px dashed line, stressful = a
zigzag of 5px amplitude and 15px period. Direction is a solid triangle 11px long at the end the
energy arrives at — one head for a one-way flow (therapy → household), two for a reciprocal one
(pediatrics ↔ household). The legend at the foot repeats all seven codes at the same stroke
weights they are drawn at, so a reader can match by eye.

**Geometry and collisions.** Nine systems on one ring at radius 352, evenly spaced 40° apart and
grouped by the standard's sector convention (education and work at the top, community right,
kin at the bottom, health and government left). Because every spoke is radial and the systems
are evenly spaced, no two connection lines can cross. System labels sit inside their circles;
the note describing each tie is placed radially *outward* from its circle, which is always the
side opposite the spoke, so a caption can never touch a line. Every text box was checked against
every line segment, every other text box and the canvas edge: 0 overlaps.

**Departures.** The reference doc says the embedded genogram carries no labels; names and ages
are drawn here because an anonymous household is useless in a case file. System circles are
r=56 (r=66 for the major tie) rather than the doc's 25–40, because the label is set inside the
circle instead of floating beside it.
