## Scenario

Estate attorneys often need to explain control, economics, and fiduciary roles on one page. This structure separates voting/control relationships from ownership and beneficiary distributions.

## Annotation key

- `trust` renders as an ellipse, distinct from corporate entities.
- `==>` marks voting or control without economic ownership.
- `-->` marks distributions to beneficiaries.
