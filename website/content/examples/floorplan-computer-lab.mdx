## What this shows

A single `grid desk-chair` lays out the lab: six columns, four rows, `count 24` truncating row-major so the last cells are dropped cleanly rather than overflowing. Each `desk-chair` draws a desk with its chair tucked in front.

The `whiteboard` sits flush to the front wall as a thin solid bar, the instructor `desk` anchors the corner, and `window … east type sliding` brings daylight down one side — every piece checked to stay inside the room.
