## Scenario

Chronograms use branch lengths as time. For variant surveillance, that means the horizontal distance conveys approximate divergence time rather than only genetic distance.

## Annotation key

- `mode: chronogram` and `mrsd` tell the renderer how to interpret branch lengths.
- `clade` highlights named groups from the Newick leaves.
- `scale` labels the time axis.
