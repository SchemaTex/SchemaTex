## Scenario

Assisted reproduction cases need more than a simple parent-child line. This example keeps the clinical pedigree compact while labeling the relevant participants: intended parents, donor, surrogate, embryo, and proband.

## Annotation key

- `pregnancy` marks the embryo node.
- `evaluated` indicates participants evaluated in the genetics intake.
- Re-declaring `child` preserves the proband while documenting alternate family context.
