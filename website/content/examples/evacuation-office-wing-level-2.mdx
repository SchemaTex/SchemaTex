## Scenario

Level 2 of Meridian House, a small office wing: ten rooms along a 22 m corridor, with a protected stair at each end. The plan is posted in the Open Office. It shows the way out to the east stair, the west stair as the alternative, and the walk from the east exit to the assembly point in the car park.

## Annotation key

- `compliance iso` and `sheet a3 landscape` — check the plan against ISO 23601 and fit it to an A3 sheet
- `show furniture` — draw furniture such as the stair flights
- `room`, `door between`, `opening between` — the floor layout and how rooms connect
- `fire-door between corridor stairW rating "EI30"` — marks a fire-rated door
- `here in openoffice ... "YOU ARE HERE"` — the reader's position
- `exit-final ... side west` — a final exit sign on the named side of a room
- `exit-direction ... hand left` — an exit sign with a direction arrow
- `extinguisher ... class "ABC"`, `call-point`, `first-aid` — safety equipment signs
- `assembly muster outside at 31,12.6` — the assembly point outside the building
- `route primary ...` / `route secondary ...` — escape routes listed room by room to an exit
- `legend auto` — a legend listing only the symbols actually used

## How to read

Find the "you are here" marker in the Open Office. The primary route leaves through the office door, turns east along the corridor, and goes down Stair 2 and out, then on to the assembly point. If that way is blocked, the secondary route runs west to Stair 1. Both stairs sit behind fire doors. Extinguishers and the call point are along the corridor, and first aid is in the Break Room.
