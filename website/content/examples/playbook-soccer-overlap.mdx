The **overlap** combines the winger and full-back: the winger drives inside (`dribble`) to fix the defender while the full-back `run`s outside and overlaps. The release pass down the line (`pass 7 to 90,8`) sets up a byline cross to the strikers attacking the box. Drawn with `view half` and explicit players for full control.
