  - Faster onboarding

  - Annual price uplift
  - Less discounting

## Scenario

A leadership team breaks the goal "Grow Revenue 30% Next Year" into the four levers that together make up revenue growth: win new customers, expand existing accounts, keep the customers we have, and raise price realization. Each lever splits into three sub-levers, and several of those name one or two first moves. The levers do not overlap, so every item has exactly one place in the tree.

## Annotation key

- `%% style: logic-right` — draw the map as a left-to-right logic chart instead of a radial map
- Other `%%` lines — comments; they are not drawn
- `# Grow Revenue 30% Next Year` — the root, on the far left
- `## Win new customers` — a first-level branch (a lever); each gets its own colour
- `- More qualified pipeline` — a second-level item under the lever above it
- Indented `  - Partner referrals` — a third-level item under the bullet above it

## How to read

Read from left to right: each column is a more specific answer to the column before it. The first column is the goal, the second the four levers, the third the sub-levers, the fourth the first moves. Every item keeps its lever's colour, so you can trace any first move — such as "Deal desk above 15% off" — back through "Less discounting" to "Raise price realization".
