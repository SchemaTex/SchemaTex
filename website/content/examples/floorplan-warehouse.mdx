## What this shows

Sixteen `pallet-rack` bays come from one `grid`, the wide column pitch opening the cross-aisles a forklift needs — and a `forklift` parked in the pick aisle proves the clearance is real, not assumed.

Two `loading-dock` symbols sit on the rear wall above their exterior doors, and a `Shipping Office` annex hangs off the south wall via `below` — an L-shaped building footprint the engine dimensions on the outside.
