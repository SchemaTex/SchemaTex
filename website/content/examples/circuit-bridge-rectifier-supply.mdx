## Scenario

A technician needs a quick schematic for the textbook AC-to-DC rectifier: a transformer secondary, four rectifier diodes, a reservoir capacitor, and a load. The netlist form keeps the topology precise while the renderer chooses the schematic placement.

## Annotation key

- `D1` to `D4` are ordinary diode components inferred from the `D` prefix.
- `0` is the canonical ground net.
- `C1` smooths the rectified output across `vout` and ground.

## How to read

During each half-cycle, two diodes conduct and steer current into `vout` with the same polarity. The capacitor charges near the peak and supplies the load between peaks, reducing ripple at `Rload`.
