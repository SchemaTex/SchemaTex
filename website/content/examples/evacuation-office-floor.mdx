## Why this plan is reviewable

Both routes name every room they cross and finish at different final exits. The engine verifies the shared openings, computes the A3 print scale, and derives the Tier-M legend from the route styles, exits, extinguishers, first-aid sign, call point, assembly point, and fire-door mark actually used.
