## What this shows

Eighteen corner workstations come from one `grid desk-l` — each L-desk draws its own task chair, and the grid's `area` corners are the centres of the first and last desk, so the 2.5 m column pitch leaves a clear circulation lane between rows.

Two service rooms hang off the open floor with `right-of`/`below` chaining; the `conference-table` and `round-table-4` auto-seat their edges, and the validator guarantees nothing — locker run, filing, fridge — strays past a wall or into a desk.
