**Four Verticals** floods a two-deep coverage with four vertical threats: the outside receivers run `go` routes up the sidelines while the inner two bend to the `seam`, forcing the two deep safeties to choose. The back leaks to the `flat` as a checkdown. Drawn out of `spread` against `cover-2`, with each route on the football route tree.
