## Scenario

Commercial PV one-lines have a recognizable chain: DC array, combiner, inverter, AC disconnect, meter, and point of interconnection. This example uses standard SLD equipment while preserving PV-specific ratings in labels.

## Annotation key

- `solar` marks the generation source.
- `hub` stands in for the combiner point.
- `watthour_meter` captures the production metering requirement.
