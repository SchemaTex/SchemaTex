## Scenario

Commercial PV one-lines have a recognizable chain: parallel DC arrays, combiner, isolator, inverter, AC protection, meter, and point of interconnection. The Utility feeder lands independently on the switchboard instead of masquerading as another step in the PV conversion chain.

## Annotation key

- `solar` marks the generation source.
- Repeated `solar -> hub` connections create a real fan-in with one cable label per source drop.
- `hub` stands in for the combiner point.
- `watthour_meter` captures the production metering requirement.
