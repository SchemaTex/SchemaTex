## Scenario

From the Horns set — 1 at the top, 4 and 5 at the elbows, 2 and 3 in the corners — against man-to-man defense. 5 steps up and screens for 1, then rolls to the rim while 4 pops to the left slot. 1 dribbles off the screen to the right wing. When X2 leaves the corner to stop the roller, 1 throws the skip pass to 2 for an open corner three.

## Annotation key

- `sport basketball` — half-court surface and basketball line conventions
- `set horns` — places the five offensive players in the Horns alignment
- `defense man` — adds a defender (X1–X5) guarding each player
- `screen 5 1` — 5 sets a screen for 1, drawn as a line ending in a T-bar
- `dribble 1 to rwing` — a dribble to a court landmark (right wing), drawn as a wavy line
- `cut 5 rim`, `cut 4 lslot` — player movement without the ball, drawn as a solid line
- `cut X2 lblock` — a defender's movement, here X2 sliding to the left block
- `pass 1 2` — a pass, drawn as a dashed line

## How to read

Follow the actions in order: the screen, the dribble, the roll and pop, then the defender's rotation, and finally the pass. In basketball diagrams a solid line is a player moving and a dashed line is the ball — the opposite of soccer diagrams — and the legend says so. The key moment is X2's move toward the lane: it leaves 2 alone in the corner, which is why the pass goes there.
