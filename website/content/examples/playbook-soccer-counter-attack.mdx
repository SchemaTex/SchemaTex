On the **counter**, speed beats numbers. The pivot wins it and finds a midfielder (`pass 6 8`) who carries into space (`dribble`), then releases a `pass` through ball as all three forwards sprint in behind (`run`). The solid pass + dashed runs read instantly as a transition moment.
