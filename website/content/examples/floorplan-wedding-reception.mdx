## What this shows

Each `round-table-8` draws its 8 chairs on the circumference (60″ top per banquet-industry convention), so 15 tables = 120 covers, countable in the SVG. The two grids and one row place all fifteen tables in three statements.

The collision validator checks **chair-ring envelopes**, not table discs — squeeze the grid `centers` span by a meter and it reports one quantified array-spacing conflict. The head table seats one side only, facing the room.
