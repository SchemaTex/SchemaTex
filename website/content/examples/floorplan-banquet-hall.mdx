## What this shows

Twelve `round-table-10` come from two `grid` statements — each table auto-seats ten chairs on its circumference, and the chair-ring envelope keeps the service aisles honest: pull two tables together and the validator reports the overlap in metres.

The hero pieces — `stage`, the hatched `dance-floor` underlay, `dj-booth`, `bar`, and a `counter` doubling as the buffet line — are placed individually, while the dual `door … south type double` gives the room its two egress points.
