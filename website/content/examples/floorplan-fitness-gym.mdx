## What this shows

`treadmill`, `weight-bench`, `power-rack`, and `yoga-mat` are the gym symbol set. Three `grid` statements zone the main floor — a cardio row along the north wall, a free-weights bank, and two power racks — while the benches' and racks' loaded-barbell envelopes reserve the lifting clearance.

The mirrored studio is a `grid yoga-mat` (an underlay, like a rug, so nothing collides with it), and the locker room runs two banks of `lockers` — each zone chained off the main floor with `right-of`/`below`.
