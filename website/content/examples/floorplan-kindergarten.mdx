## What this shows

The chair arc around the kidney table is one statement — `arc chair … from 35 to 145` — with each chair rotated to face the center. The kidney table itself is the standard early-ed reading-group shape.

Zoning reads at a glance: wet play along the north counter, group tables mid-room, the rug-marked circle-time corner (an underlay, so furniture could sit on it), and the nap corner with cribs kept clear of the east door swing.
