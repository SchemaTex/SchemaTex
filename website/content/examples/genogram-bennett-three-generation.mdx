## Scenario

A family therapist's case-file genogram. Chloe, 18, is the index person and has been referred for depression. The chart covers three generations: her grandparents Harold (died 2016) and Margaret, her father Daniel and his sister Susan, her mother Karen, and Daniel's second wife Priya. It answers the four questions a clinician asks first: who has died, who separated from whom, which children belong to which union, and where the emotional tension sits.

## Annotation key

- `[male, 1938, deceased, death: 2016]` — sex, birth year, and death marker with year
- `label: "Harold"` — the name printed under the symbol
- `conditions: alcohol-use(half-left, #FCD34D)` — a condition drawn as a coloured fill over part of the symbol
- `index` — marks Chloe as the person the case is about
- `--` with `"m. 1965"` — a marriage line with its date
- `-x-` — a divorced couple; the label carries both the marriage and divorce years
- Indented people under a couple — that couple's children
- `-fused-`, `-conflict-`, `-cutoff-`, `-close-` — emotional relationship lines between two people

## How to read

Start at the top: Harold (crossed out, deceased) and Margaret, with their children Daniel and Susan below. Daniel appears in two unions — the divorced marriage to Karen, which produced Chloe and Ethan, and the later marriage to Priya, which produced Ravi. The emotional lines then show the tension around Chloe: fused with her mother, in conflict with her father, and close to her brother; separately, Margaret has cut off contact with Susan. The shared alcohol-use fill on Harold and Daniel shows a pattern repeating across generations.
