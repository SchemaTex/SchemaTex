**Horns** places two bigs at the elbows and two shooters in the corners. One big screens for the ball (`screen 5 1`) and rolls to the `rim` while the second pops to the top (`cut 4`), giving the ball-handler a roll-or-pop read plus a corner skip (`pass 1 3`).
