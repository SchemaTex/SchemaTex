## Scenario

The ground floor of Linden House, a small two-storey home: living room, open kitchen and dining, a guest bedroom, a hall with the stair to the upper floor, a bathroom and a utility room. Six rooms, 92.0 m². The plan uses the whole residential drawing kit — two wall thicknesses, several door types, windows, a stair, and kitchen and bathroom fixtures.

## Annotation key

- `unit m` and `north` — metres, with a north arrow
- `wall exterior thickness 0.30` / `wall interior thickness 0.12` — the two wall weights
- `room id "Name" at x,y size WxH` — a room placed by its corner; `right-of` places it beside another room
- `door hall south from start 0.25 width 0.95 id front hinge left swing in` — an outside door on one wall of a room, with its position, hinge side and swing
- `door between A B at 50% width 1.4 type double` — a door in the wall two rooms share; `type pocket` and `type sliding` change the symbol
- `opening between living kitchen` — a doorway with no door
- `window room side at 40% width 2.0` — a window on one wall of a room
- `furniture kind in room at x,y size WxH rotate 180` — a fixture or furniture piece placed inside a room; `stairs` is placed the same way

## How to read

North is up. The living room and kitchen/dining face the garden to the north and share an open doorway; a sliding door leads from the dining area outside. The front door on the south wall opens into the hall, which holds the stair and reaches the living room through double doors, the guest bedroom through a hinged door, and the bathroom through a pocket door. The utility room connects to the kitchen and has its own side door to the outside.
