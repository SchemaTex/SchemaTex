## Scenario

A logistics director picks the site for a second distribution centre. Four cities — Reno, Salt Lake City, Phoenix and Boise — are scored from 1 to 5 against five criteria, each weighted by importance. Reno, the current favourite, is the baseline that every other option is compared against.

## Annotation key

- `mode: decision` — a weighted decision matrix: the engine multiplies each score by its weight and adds them up
- `baseline: "Reno"` — the reference option; every other option shows its difference from it
- `option "..."` — one column per candidate
- `criterion "..." weight: 5` — one row per criterion, with its importance
- `Reno: 3` — under a criterion, the score each option gets

## How to read

Each column's total is the sum of score × weight. Salt Lake City wins with 77, 17 points ahead of Reno's 60, mainly on labour availability and truck reach. Phoenix is second with 67 despite a score of 1 on heat and water risk. Boise comes last at 57 — the cheapest land, but weak on the two most heavily weighted criteria. Change a weight or a score and the ranking is recomputed.
