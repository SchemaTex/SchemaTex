## Scenario

Data centers are sold on redundancy. A 2N power path has two independent sources and UPS chains so either side can carry the critical load. A one-line diagram makes that redundancy auditable.

## Annotation key

- Two utility and transformer paths stay separate until the transfer point.
- `ups` nodes show the energy-storage conversion stage.
- The transfer switch feeds two PDUs before the critical IT load.
