Inside the 5-yard line the field changes: `goal 5` draws the end zone band, gold goal line, and goalposts. The play fakes the `dive` (`handoff` + `run`) to freeze the linebackers, then throws the back-corner `fade` with a `slant` and `out` as alternatives against `cover-1`.
