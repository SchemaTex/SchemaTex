## What this shows

`grid row-chairs rows 6 cols 2` lays out both seating banks in one statement; each strip auto-places chairs at the 0.55 m theater pitch, so the plan's seat count (8 per strip × 12 = 96) is structural, not decorative — count the chair elements in the SVG.

The two double doors at the rear and the side door give code-style egress, and the aisle width is just the gap between the grid's `area` columns — widen it by editing one x-coordinate.
