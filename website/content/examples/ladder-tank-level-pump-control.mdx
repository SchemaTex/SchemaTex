    parallel:
      branch:
        XIC(LOW_LEVEL, "IN 1.0", name="Low level")
      branch:
        XIC(PUMP_RUN, "OUT 2.0", name="Pump running")
    XIO(HIGH_LEVEL, "IN 1.1", name="High level")
    XIO(FAULT, "BIT 3.0", name="Fault clear")
    OTE(PUMP_RUN, "OUT 2.0", name="Fill pump")

  rung 2 "Run delay proves flow":
    XIC(PUMP_RUN, "OUT 2.0", name="Pump running")
    TON(FLOW_DELAY, IN=PUMP_RUN, PT=5000, name="Flow prove delay")

  rung 3 "No-flow alarm after delay":
    XIC(FLOW_DELAY, "TMR 1.0", name="Delay done")
    XIO(FLOW_OK, "IN 1.2", name="Flow switch")
    OTL(FLOW_ALARM, "BIT 3.1", name="No flow alarm")

## Scenario

A fill pump starts when the tank reaches low level, stays on through a seal-in path, and stops at the high-level switch. A timer allows a few seconds for the flow switch to prove flow before latching an alarm.

## Annotation key

- The `parallel:` block is the seal-in path.
- `TON` is the on-delay timer used as a permissive before declaring no-flow.
- `OTL` latches the alarm so an operator must acknowledge the failure.
