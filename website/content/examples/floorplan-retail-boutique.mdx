## What this shows

`shelving` (gondola runs), `checkout`, `clothing-rack`, and `fitting-room` are the retail symbol set. The sales-floor gondolas come from one `grid`, leaving 1.5 m aisles, while two free-standing round racks bracket the floor.

The stockroom and fitting area chain off the sales floor with `right-of`/`below`; the three changing booths sit in a `grid fitting-room` with a waiting `sofa` opposite — and the collision check keeps the aisles walkable.
