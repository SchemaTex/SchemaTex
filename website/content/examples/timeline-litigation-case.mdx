## Scenario

Litigation timelines mix hard deadlines, long phases, and high-stakes milestones. The lollipop style keeps single events readable while still allowing discovery to appear as a range.

## Annotation key

- `config: style = lollipop` selects the narrative timeline style.
- Date ranges render as spans.
- `milestone` emphasizes critical procedural events.
