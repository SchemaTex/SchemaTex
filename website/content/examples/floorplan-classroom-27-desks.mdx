## What this shows

`grid desk-chair … count 27` places a 5×6 grid and drops the last row's tail — exactly how a real 27-student room fills. The `centers` coordinates explicitly name the first and last item centers, so respacing the whole room is a two-number edit.

`unit ft` makes every number feet: the dimension strings render as 32′/26′ and the (suppressed) room area would be in sq ft. Cubbies and supply counters use the underlay convention so back-row desks may legitimately touch them.
