## What this shows

A site plan has no nested rooms — the lot is **tiled** by non-overlapping zones (`front`, `house`, `drive`, `yard`) that share edges, exactly as the engine models adjacency. The `Residence` footprint stays an empty hatched box, the way a permit set shows the building outline.

`tree` and `car` are sized for the outdoors — trees in feet across the yards, two cars filling the driveway stalls — and `door between house drive` resolves the garage opening onto the shared wall. The engine prints the exterior dimension string in feet-and-inches.
