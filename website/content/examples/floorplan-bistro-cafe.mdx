## What this shows

The booth tables are `dining-table … rotate 90` — their auto-seated chairs rotate with them, and the collision check uses the rotated envelopes (separating-axis test), so the validator stays accurate at any angle.

The kitchen shows the commercial line conventions: counter run with dashed wall cabinets over it, range hood over the stove, and a prep island. The restroom pair places a urinal and keeps fixtures clear of the door swings.
