3 — Repeated / shared event (absorption)

MSF feeds BOTH G1 and G2 — drawn twice with the shared-event mark. MOCUS applies absorption: {MSF,ESF} and {MSF,RCF} are dropped as supersets of {MSF}. Minimal cut sets: {MSF}, {CDM} — both SPOFs. This is where naive shape stencils get the math wrong.
